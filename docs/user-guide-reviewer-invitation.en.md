# Inviting a Reviewer - User Guide

*[English](user-guide-reviewer-invitation.en.md) · [Français](user-guide-reviewer-invitation.fr.md)*

## Overview

The **Invite a reviewer** page (button on the paper's administration page) helps you choose whom to invite:

- for each person, it shows what they are **already asked in the journal**, so that the same reviewers are not asked
  too often;
- on a new version of a paper, it lists the **reviewers of the previous versions** and lets you **invite some of them
  again**.

This information is only shown to the editorial team managing the paper.

## Requests in the Journal

The **Requests in the journal** column is shown in the table of the reviewers known to the journal, in the table of the
accounts found for a new reviewer, and in the table of the reviewers of the previous versions. It shows:

- **Reviews in progress**: accepted reviews not submitted yet;
- **Pending invitations**: invitations not answered yet and not expired;
- the number of the papers concerned (link to their administration page, in a new tab), with the review due date or the
  date the invitation was sent;
- **Latest invitation**: its date and the answer given (accepted, declined, cancelled, expired, or not answered yet).

Good to know:

- only papers **under review** are counted (not published, rejected papers, or papers replaced by a new version); the
  paper you are handling is not counted;
- you only see the number of the papers you may manage; the others are only counted ("Other papers: 2");
- the table lists three papers at most per person; the full list is shown at the next step.

When you choose a person, the same information is shown **above the invitation message**: you can adapt your message,
for instance to thank a reviewer who is already much asked.

This also applies to a person **without an account** already invited by the journal: type their address in **Invite a
new user**, their previous invitations are found from it.

## Inviting Again a Reviewer of a Previous Version

On a new version of a paper, the **Reviewers of the previous versions** table lists, for each previous version, the
people invited and what became of their request:

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Report submitted | the person completed their report |
| Review started | the report is in progress |
| Invitation accepted | invitation accepted, report not started |
| Invitation declined / expired / cancelled | the person did not review this version |
| Not answered | the invitation was never answered |

To invite a person again:

1. Click **Invite again** on their row.
2. The message offered is the one of the **reassignment to a new version** (a template you can customise among the
   journal's e-mail templates); it links to the version the person reviewed. Check the due date and the message, then
   send it.

The button is shown once per person. When a person may not be invited, the reason is shown instead: **Already invited
on this version**, **Author of the article**, **Yourself** or **Conflict of interest declared**.

The **Profile** link opens the person's profile (accounts only).

> To invite **all** the reviewers again automatically when the new version is submitted, still use the
> **Automatically reassign the same reviewers when the new version of the article is submitted** option of the
> revision request. The table is for inviting only some of them, or
> doing it later.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Why is the "Requests" column of a person empty?**
They were never invited by the journal ("Never invited").

**Does the reassignment message say that the person was invited again automatically?**
No: the default text says "You are invited again to review the manuscript". If your journal customised this template,
your text is used; read it before sending.
